# Privacy and safety

The starter contains synthetic records only. It is not approved for real patient information.

Before real-world use, complete a data-flow inventory, privacy impact assessment, clinical-safety review, threat model, accessibility audit, retention schedule, incident-response plan, and jurisdiction-specific legal review. Use strong identity, least-privilege authorization, encryption in transit and at rest, managed secrets, immutable audit records, tested backups, and monitored recovery procedures.

OpenRehabOps must not be used for diagnosis, prescribing, emergency response, autonomous treatment decisions, or direct medical-device control. Operational states such as `available` or `online` must not be interpreted as clinical suitability or device safety.
